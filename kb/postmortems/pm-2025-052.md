# Postmortem: False alarm from deprecated config warnings

## Summary
A config reload began emitting "deprecated key 'retry.backoff_ms' ignored" warnings on the gateway. They were mistaken for the cause of an unrelated latency alert that was actually a payments regression.

## Lessons
- Warnings without a matching change in error_rate or latency are noise.
- Verify that a suspected cause appears before the symptom it supposedly explains.
