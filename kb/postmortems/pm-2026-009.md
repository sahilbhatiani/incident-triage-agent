# Postmortem: Payments heap leak in the retry client

## Summary
A retry client in payments kept references to failed request bodies. Heap usage climbed to 93% and the pod was OOMKilled three times in an hour. Orders saw intermittent 502s.

## Lessons
- A disk usage warning on payments during the incident was unrelated log volume.
- The mem sawtooth identified the leak in under five minutes once someone looked.
