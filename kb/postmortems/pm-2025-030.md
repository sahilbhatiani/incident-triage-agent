# Postmortem: Inventory lookups timing out during sale

## Summary
A slow external SKU service caused inventory handlers to block until the 5000ms timeout. The worker pool filled (64/64 busy) and orders retried aggressively, tripling load.

## Timeline
- 18:05 inventory p95 latency began climbing.
- 18:07 orders logged "call to inventory failed: 504 Gateway Timeout".
- 18:09 gateway alert fired.

## Lessons
- Retry storms upstream amplify, but do not cause, the incident. Look for the deepest service whose latency rose first.
